PulseLink ("we", "us", "our") operates a health information exchange that lets patients, healthcare providers, laboratories, pharmacies, and insurers securely share medical information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you stay in control of it.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use PulseLink, we may collect:

  • Account information: your name, contact details, and identity verification details.
  • Health information: lab results, prescriptions, imaging, diagnoses, immunisation records, and consultation notes, as submitted by you or your healthcare providers.
  • Insurance information: cover details relevant to claims processing, where applicable.
  • Usage information: access logs, login activity, and device information used to keep your account secure.

2. How we use it

We use your information to operate the health information exchange: to display your records to you, to route authorised information to the providers you approve, to verify prescriptions and claims, and to maintain the security and audit trail the platform depends on. We do not use your medical information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Who you control access for

You decide which providers can view your record, what parts of it they can see, and for how long. Access can be granted for a single visit or an ongoing relationship, and can be revoked at any time from your account. Some access — such as emergency access in life-threatening situations, where legally permitted — may follow a separate, narrower protocol that we'll disclose clearly if it applies to you.

4. How information is shared with providers

Providers you've authorised — hospitals, doctors, laboratories, pharmacies, or insurers — receive only the information relevant to the care or service they're providing, through an encrypted, access-controlled connection. We log every access event: who viewed what, and when.

5. How we secure it

Medical records are encrypted at rest and in transit. Access is controlled by role-based permissions specific to each type of user — patient, doctor, nurse, lab staff, pharmacist, or administrator. A cryptographic fingerprint of each record is stored on a blockchain so that any tampering with a record can be detected immediately; the record's actual contents are never stored on-chain.

6. Retention & deletion

We retain health records for as long as your account is active, and for any additional period required by applicable medical record-keeping law. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time; some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate audit and safety purposes, and we'll explain what applies to your specific request.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent for data sharing at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's data

Where a patient is a minor, their health record is managed by a parent or legal guardian until local law permits the minor to manage it directly. Guardians control access on the minor's behalf during this period.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page whenever this policy changes, and where changes are material, we'll notify account holders directly before they take effect.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@pulselink.health, or through our contact page.

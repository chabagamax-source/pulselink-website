Common questions about PulseLink.

For patients

For providers

Hospitals, clinics, laboratories, pharmacies, and insurers. Every institution goes through a verification process before it can request or share patient data.

Security & trust

No. Your actual medical record is encrypted and stored in a secure database. Only a cryptographic fingerprint of the record is written to the blockchain, so tampering can be detected without ever exposing your data.

Every access to every record is logged and auditable, which means unauthorized access is both harder to achieve and easier to detect. Full detail on our security model and breach procedures is in our Privacy Policy.

You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time, subject to legal and medical record-keeping requirements outlined in our Privacy Policy.
